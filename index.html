Four steps to protect yourself

Pause

Do not rush, even if the message says it is an emergency.

Keep details private

Never share passwords, card PINs or SMS verification codes.

Check independently

Use the official app, website or phone number you find yourself.

Ask for help

Talk to a trusted person or contact the organisation directly.
