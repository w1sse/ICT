TRUSTED GUIDANCE

You do not have to handle it alone.

If you are unsure about a call, message or website, stop and ask someone you trust. Use official contact channels instead of details provided by a suspicious sender.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I clicked a suspicious link?

Close the page. Do not enter information or download files. If you entered a password, change it using the official website or app. If you shared banking details or a verification code, contact your bank immediately. If you downloaded a file, seek trusted technical support.

What if someone already knows my SMS code?

Contact your bank or the service involved immediately through official contact details. Tell them what happened and follow their instructions. Do not share additional codes.

Can Cyber-Shield guarantee that a website is safe?

No. This is an educational prototype. Its local checks only look for common warning signs. Reliable reputation checks require maintained threat-intelligence services and a secure backend.

Does Cyber-Shield store my information?

This prototype runs checks in your browser and does not send the entered address or IP to a server. Avoid entering private information anyway.

What if the caller sounds exactly like someone I know?

Voice and video can be imitated. Hang up and contact the person using a different number or a communication method you already trust. Do not send money until you verify the request.
